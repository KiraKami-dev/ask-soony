# Questions for Soony

We get only a few questions, so this list keeps the ones the data cannot answer and whose answer changes what we build. Most important first.

1. **Game cards are about 6,800 short. Which edition gets them first, and what does a missing copy cost on launch day?**
   Hwaseong's air shipment left with only 28,000 of the 48,000 cards; the other 20,000 arrive Tue 24 Nov. Both editions need the same card. We need the cost of a missing Standard copy vs. a missing Collector's copy to decide where the cards go, and whether extra air freight or overtime is worth paying for.

2. **Which GH6 sleeve artwork is released: rev. B or rev. C?**
   Karton Nowak prints rev. C ("received from Soony Marketing 2026-08-07"). PackRight prints rev. B, which matches Soony's master data. Hamburg holds 122,000 rev. C and 64,000 rev. B sleeves against 128,000 needed. If only rev. B is valid, the Standard line is about 64,000 sleeves short. If both are acceptable, two designs go to retail.

3. **Who decides for Collector's Items while the Category Lead role is empty?**
   Under policy PP-07 that role does three things:
   - approves Collector's air freight
   - signs quality concessions, such as for the failed statue lot
   - extends the conditional release of Pune Diecast Works, whose two statue orders are stuck in "awaiting approval"

   We need to know who our agent should escalate to.

4. **Can Hamburg physically count the art books, and confirm whether statue lot 5000413063 is blocked?**
   - **Art books:** the delivery note WZ-261116-473 says 8,000, with 80,000 crossed out by hand, and the order was for 8,000. SAP booked 80,000. If the note is right, art books are about 5,900 short instead of "ok".
   - **Statues:** lot 5000413063 (5,600 statues) failed incoming inspection but sits in usable stock. Without it, statues are about 3,400 short.

   The launch plan treats both parts as fine.

5. **Can Hamburg add hours on the weekend (a night shift or a longer Sunday)?**
   The Collector's line needs 28 of its 30 hours. Any late part means lost copies unless capacity can stretch.
