Summary
The idea. A layer on top of Soony's procurement process that finds what threatens the launch deadline and alerts the right person: a phone call if it is critical, otherwise an email or a notification in the portal.

Two error sources to tackle:

Three-way match (main focus): compare the purchase order in SAP, the supplier's order confirmation (PDF, in its own language) and what Hamburg actually received (scanned delivery note, sometimes with handwritten corrections like "100" crossed out, "80" written over it).
Shipment tracking and prediction: predict whether a shipment arrives in time, using past patterns, the shipment data and news drops (weather, strikes, etc.), and propose alternatives when it won't (e.g. "this supplier can only deliver 500 of 1,000, here's a draft mail to another supplier, click send").
Criticality. For every problem: can it wait two or three hours (notification), or must someone act now (call)? The deadline per order comes from the promised date the buyer enters with the PO.

Automation and trust. Start by asking a human; let them allow an action "this time" or "always" (like a permissions prompt). Internal actions can be automatic sooner; anything sent to suppliers gets stricter rules.
