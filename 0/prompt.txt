**Rules** (in `src/ask_soony/papercheck.py`, one function each, all found automatically):
- **(a) Arrives late or short:** a document says less was shipped than the open PO line, or that the rest arrives after the promised date or after launch.
- **(b) Old revision confirmed:** a document confirms or ships a part to a revision the latest engineering change says must not be packed. Open PO lines are amber; parts already in stock are taken out of the stock count.
- **(c) Stock booked wrong:** the delivery note quantity (handwritten corrections read by the vision model) differs from the goods receipt. Without the LLM, a receipt exactly 10x or 1/10 of the order is flagged, with the scan attached for checking.
- **(d) Failed lot counted as stock:** an inspection report rejects a lot whose batch is still in stock as usable.
- **(e) Held at supplier:** a document says the goods are on hold, refused or suspended while SAP shows the PO open or the shipment moving.

A card is red when it leaves a launch edition short: remaining need is compared with stock plus PO lines due before launch, with and without the contradicted quantity. Otherwise it is amber. Launch time comes from the launch plan's last shift (Sun 20:00), or `ASK_LAUNCH_AT`.

**How to run:**
- `uv run python -m ask_soony.papercheck` writes the cards; add `--dry-run` to only print, `--no-llm` for deterministic only.
- `uv run python scripts/demo_cards.py --reset` keeps the old reset (backups, clears decisions, routines off), removes the old hand-written `gr-*`, `rev-*` and `qa-*` entries, then runs the paper check. No document references are left in the script.
- With `ASK_BACKGROUND=on`, `background.start()` runs it at startup and again whenever the portal mirror changes (`ASK_PAPERCHECK=off` disables it).
- Merging is by id: other entries stay, `decision_status.json` is never touched, and notes other agents added to a card are kept. A paper-check card whose contradiction disappears is marked resolved.
